the missing asset

The decision traces experts learn from

Were never logged.

Companies log documents and outcomes — not rejected alternatives, assumptions, counterfactuals, or why an expert chose A over B. The training distribution for specialist judgment largely does not exist as a usable dataset — in pretraining or in enterprise logs.

why ordinary synthetic data is not enough

More data is not
more coverage.

Self-Instruct — more task variety · Evol-Instruct — harder examples · Persona-driven — more perspectives
Self-play — improve against yourself · Adversarial — find failures · Distillation — copy a teacher
All useful operators. Ours is the layer above them: a closed loop over the business decision space.

the seed experiment · to prove

Does verified coverage
become expertise?

Hypothesis — at matched compute and base model, higher verified coverage ⇒ higher held-out specialist performance.
Against — frontier + best harness · matched SFT · equal-budget teacher distillation.
Judged on — decision quality · critical errors · calibration · cost. Thresholds defined before training.

Appendix · before you ask

The questions you're already asking.

"Won't the next frontier model make this pointless?"

Better base models make our specialists stronger — we retrain on our data. The moat is the coverage data, not the weights.

"Is synthetic data any good?"

Seed-bound synthetic data circles what it already knows. Ours expands a mapped space with measured coverage.

"Why won't the labs build this?"

They sell generality. And they're buyers of this layer — $1B+/year — not just rivals.

"How do you measure a CEO model?"

Outcomes take years — the decision process is checkable now: facts, constraints, risks, assumptions. That's how boards judge CEOs.

"What if a lab copies the method?"

Every cycle adds coverage nobody else has. A copy starts at zero on a curve we publish.

"What about hallucinations?"

In a generator, imagination is the point — a scenario is a region to master, not a fact to memorize.

"Where's revenue?"

This seed buys the proof; first paid deployments land with design partners around the next round.

"Why this team?"

Training (Gagan) + inference cost (Nitin) + live companies (Avinash). The thesis is the three of us.
